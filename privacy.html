Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 August 2026

ROAMTALLY is designed as a local-first travel planning app. You do not need to create a ROAMTALLY account to use the app.

Travel data

Trip history, planned trips, traveler profiles, passport-country selections, rule settings and related planning data are stored on your device. ROAMTALLY does not operate a server account that receives this travel history as part of normal app use.

Apple features

If you choose to add a trip or deadline to Apple Calendar, ROAMTALLY requests the relevant Calendar permission and writes the event you requested. If you enable alerts, the app schedules local notifications. Widgets, Live Activities and supported Apple experiences may receive a limited travel-status snapshot through Apple-provided app-sharing mechanisms so those features can display your status.

Purchases

App Store purchases are processed by Apple. ROAMTALLY uses RevenueCat to determine whether PRO access is active. RevenueCat and the app store may process purchase, transaction, anonymous app-user and device/app information necessary to provide and restore entitlements under their own privacy terms.

Location

ROAMTALLY does not require continuous location tracking to calculate travel-day allowances. Travel entries are provided by the user.

Data sales and advertising

ROAMTALLY does not sell your travel data and does not use it for third-party advertising.

Your choices

You can delete or change travel information from the app and can remove the app to remove locally stored app data, subject to device backups managed by your platform. Calendar events created at your request can be removed from Calendar.

Important limitation

ROAMTALLY is a planning tool and not legal or immigration advice. Always verify important travel requirements with the relevant official authority.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, visit ROAMTALLY Support.
