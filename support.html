ROAMTALLY Support

ROAMTALLY helps you track travel days, stay allowances, passport-aware travel rules and planned trips.

Before requesting support

Check that your trip entry and exit dates are correct. If reminders or calendar features are not working, review the relevant permissions in iOS Settings. If ROAMTALLY PRO is not recognized after a previous purchase, use Restore Purchases in the app.

Travel-rule questions

ROAMTALLY is a planning tool and does not provide legal or immigration advice. Always verify visa, passport, residence and border-entry requirements with the relevant official authority before travel.

Contact support

You can open a support request in the official ROAMTALLY repository:

Open a ROAMTALLY support request
